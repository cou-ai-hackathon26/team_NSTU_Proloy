# Shoi (সই) — Crisis Safety & Medical Guardrails Protocol

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Principle (স্ক্রিনিং, ডায়াগনোসিস নয়)
- Shoi is an educational and screening companion, **never** a certified doctor or diagnostic tool.
- PCOS results always output as a "risk indicator" with mandatory referrals to registered gynecologists.
- The assistant strictly refrains from prescribing medications or drug dosages (e.g., specifying milligrams of any drug).

## 2. Crisis & Self-Harm Layered Protocol
When a user exhibits acute emotional distress or self-harm ideation:
1. **Layer 1 (Direct Lexicon):** Direct suicide or self-harm keywords trigger immediate High Risk.
2. **Layer 2 (Colloquial & Banglish):** Dialectical despair and romanized phrases trigger High/Medium Risk.
3. **Safety Intervention Payload:**
   - The user is **never** subjected to an automated or silent call (which could cause alarm or domestic complications).
   - Instead, the app renders a soft, calming support screen featuring:
     - Prominent direct call button for **৯৯৯ (জাতীয় জরুরি সেবা)** (`tel:999`).
     - Secondary button for **১০৯ (নারী ও শিশু নির্যাতন প্রতিরোধ হেল্পলাইন)** (`tel:109`).
     - Grounding breathing exercise.
     - Option to alert a trusted on-device contact.

## 3. Maternal Emergency Guardrails
If pregnancy reports contain any of the 5 danger signs (তীব্র রক্তক্ষরণ, খিঁচুনি, তীব্র মাথাব্যথা/ঝাপসা দৃষ্টি, পানি ভাঙা, তীব্র জ্বর, শিশুর নড়াচড়া হ্রাস), the AI provides an urgent, bold escalation message commanding immediate hospital evaluation.
